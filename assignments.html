Projects & assessments

Build, analyze, defend.

Three group projects progress from a two-modality continuous-model study to a discrete-model workshop paper and an ICML-ready contribution. Official instructions and submissions live in Canvas.

Submission policy: Code and writeups are due at 8:30 a.m. on the listed date. Each project concludes with a 30-minute defense: a 10-minute presentation followed by 20 minutes of questions.

Written exams

Two closed-book written exams, worth 15% each, assess mathematical reasoning, derivations, stochastic-process analysis, and algorithm design. Students may use one reference sheet.

  • Exam 1 — Thursday, October 8: material taught August 25 through September 29.
  • Exam 2 — Thursday, November 19: cumulative material taught August 25 through November 12.

Practice problems

There are no graded problem sets. Practice problems with complete solutions will be released during the semester to support exam preparation and fluency with course derivations.
