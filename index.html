About the course

From probability paths to biological sequence design.

The course connects mathematical derivations to working algorithms. Students study continuous models first, develop their discrete counterparts, and then connect generative modeling to optimal transport and stochastic control.

01

Build foundations

Probability, linear algebra, differential equations, information theory, Markov chains, and the master equation.

02

Derive frameworks

Continuous and discrete diffusion, flow matching, autoregressive models, and neural parametrizations.

03

Design and evaluate

Implement models, run benchmarks and ablations, and develop methods for biological sequence design.

Coursework

Research-shaped work with rigorous defenses.

Three group projects progress from an empirical workshop paper to a novel main-conference-style contribution. Two written exams assess mathematical reasoning and algorithm design.
